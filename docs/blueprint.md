# **App Name**: Silvio.AI Portfolio

## Core Features

- **Dynamic Theming & Visual System**: Six selectable accent themes (`Synth Purple` default, `Electric Blue`, `Cyber Lime`, `Crimson`, `Amber`, `Rose`) persisted in local storage alongside an adaptive animated `BinaryBackground`, glassmorphism navbar, and editorial + technical typography (`Space Grotesk`, `Inter`, `Instrument Serif`, and `JetBrains Mono`).
- **Interactive Navigation & Command Interfaces**: Global Command Palette (`Ctrl + K`) for instant navigation and quick actions, plus an Interactive CLI Terminal modal (`Ctrl + .` to toggle, `Esc` to close) supporting `help`, `whoami`, `ls`, `grep`, `certs`, and live AI queries.
- **Featured Case Studies & RAG Benchmark (`/projects`)**: Architecture pipeline breakdowns and trade-off notes for 6 flagship projects, paired with an empirical `RAG Architecture Benchmark` comparing Vector, Graph, and Hybrid RRF retrieval across latency, tokens, and USD cost.
- **65-Repository Catalog & Subgrid Comparator (`/repository`)**: Searchable catalog of 65 open-source repositories across `GenAI`, `Backend`, `Automation`, `Data Science`, `Analytics`, `Vibe Coding`, and `Fun`, with clickable tech tags, a `Live Demo Only` filter, CSS-subgrid side-by-side comparison (`2` or `3` repos), and cached GitHub stats via `/api/github-stats`.
- **Skills Radar, Role Matcher & Verified Archive (`/skills`)**: 9-axis `Core Competency Map` (`RadarChart`) and `Domain Proficiency Breakdown` (including `Automation`, `Vibe Coding`, and `Deployment`), curated `Role Fit & Specialization Matcher`, searchable `Tech Stack & Tools` grid, and a filterable `Verified Credentials` archive (`Certifications` & `Course Badges`).
- **Live RAG Assistant, Contact & Resume Delivery (`/contact`, `/about`)**: Bilingual (`EN` / `ID`) `Silvio.AI Assistant` available both embedded on `/contact` and as a global floating drawer on other routes, alongside cross-browser `ResumePreviewFrame` image preview and `/api/resume?download=1` PDF downloads.
